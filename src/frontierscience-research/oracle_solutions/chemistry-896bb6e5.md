# Theoretical ATP yields from complete oxidation of fatty acids

## 0. Bookkeeping conventions used throughout

Stated assumptions: **1 NADH → 3 ATP** and **1 FADH$_2$ → 2 ATP** (classical/theoretical P:O ratios).

**(a) Yield per $\beta$-oxidation cycle.** Each round of $\beta$-oxidation (acyl-CoA dehydrogenase → enoyl-CoA hydratase → 3-hydroxyacyl-CoA dehydrogenase → thiolase) produces 1 FADH$_2$ and 1 NADH:

$$1\ \text{FADH}_2 \times 2 + 1\ \text{NADH}\times 3 = \mathbf{5\ \text{ATP per }\beta\text{-oxidation cycle}}$$

**(b) Yield per acetyl-CoA in the TCA cycle.** Each acetyl-CoA gives 3 NADH (isocitrate DH, $\alpha$-ketoglutarate DH, malate DH), 1 FADH$_2$ (succinate DH) and 1 GTP ≡ 1 ATP (succinyl-CoA synthetase):

$$3\times 3 + 1\times 2 + 1 = \mathbf{12\ \text{ATP per acetyl-CoA}}$$

**(c) Number of cycles and acetyl-CoA units.** For an **even**-chain saturated acyl-CoA with $n$ carbons: $n/2$ acetyl-CoA and $(n/2 - 1)$ cycles. For an **odd**-chain acyl-CoA with $n$ carbons: $\beta$-oxidation proceeds until the 5-carbon acyl-CoA is cleaved to acetyl-CoA + **propionyl-CoA**, giving $(n-3)/2$ acetyl-CoA and $(n-3)/2$ cycles.

**(d) Propionyl-CoA disposal (odd chains).** Propionyl-CoA → (propionyl-CoA carboxylase, **costs 1 ATP**, biotin) → D-methylmalonyl-CoA → (epimerase) → L-methylmalonyl-CoA → (methylmalonyl-CoA mutase, B$_{12}$) → **succinyl-CoA**. Entering the TCA cycle at succinyl-CoA and running to oxaloacetate yields:

- succinyl-CoA → succinate: **+1 GTP ≡ +1 ATP**
- succinate → fumarate: **+1 FADH$_2$**
- fumarate → malate: nothing
- malate → oxaloacetate: **+1 NADH**

Net for propionyl-CoA: $-1$ ATP (carboxylation) $+1$ ATP (succinyl-CoA synthetase) $= \mathbf{0\ \text{net ATP}}$, plus **1 FADH$_2$ + 1 NADH**.

**(e) Unsaturation.** A double bond in the acyl chain means that, for the cycle in which it lies, the $\alpha,\beta$-enoyl intermediate is generated by isomerase/reductase handling rather than by acyl-CoA dehydrogenase, so **that cycle's acyl-CoA dehydrogenase step (and therefore 1 FADH$_2$) is skipped**: subtract 1 FADH$_2$ (= 2 ATP) per double bond.

**(f) Activation.** "Activated" means the substrate is already the acyl-CoA thioester, so the activation cost has been paid. "Unactivated" means the free fatty acid must first be activated by acyl-CoA synthetase:

$$\text{RCOOH} + \text{CoASH} + \text{ATP} \rightarrow \text{RCO-SCoA} + \text{AMP} + 2\,\text{P}_i$$

Because PP$_i$ is hydrolysed, this consumes **2 ATP equivalents** (ATP → AMP): subtract 2 ATP.

## 1. Activated 18-carbon **saturated** fatty acid (stearoyl-CoA)

An 18-carbon saturated chain is cleaved 8 times to give 9 acetyl-CoA:

- acetyl-CoA produced: $18/2 = 9$
- $\beta$-oxidation cycles: $9 - 1 = 8$

Cofactor tally:

| Source | FADH$_2$ | NADH | ATP(GTP) |
|---|---|---|---|
| 8 $\beta$-oxidation cycles | 8 | 8 | 0 |
| 9 acetyl-CoA in TCA (9 × [1 FADH$_2$, 3 NADH, 1 GTP]) | 9 | 27 | 9 |
| activation cost (already activated) | – | – | 0 |
| **Total** | **17** | **35** | **9** |

**For number 1, 17 FADH$_2$ + 35 NADH + 9 ATPs are net generated.**

$$\text{ATP} = 17\times 2 + 35\times 3 + 9 = 34 + 105 + 9 = \mathbf{148}$$

Equivalently: $8\ \text{cycles}\times 5 + 9\ \text{acetyl-CoA}\times 12 = 40 + 108 = 148$.

**Final answer (1): net 148 ATP.**

---

## 2. Activated 15-carbon **saturated** fatty acid (pentadecanoyl-CoA)

Odd chain: $\beta$-oxidation runs $(15-3)/2 = 6$ cycles, producing **6 acetyl-CoA + 1 propionyl-CoA**.

| Source | FADH$_2$ | NADH | ATP(GTP) |
|---|---|---|---|
| 6 $\beta$-oxidation cycles | 6 | 6 | 0 |
| 6 acetyl-CoA in TCA | 6 | 18 | 6 |
| propionyl-CoA → succinyl-CoA → OAA | 1 | 1 | $-1 + 1 = 0$ |
| activation cost (already activated) | – | – | 0 |
| **Total** | **13** | **25** | **6** |

**For number 2, 13 FADH$_2$ + 25 NADH + 6 ATPs are net generated.**

$$\text{ATP} = 13\times 2 + 25\times 3 + 6 = 26 + 75 + 6 = \mathbf{107}$$

Equivalently: $6\times 5 + 6\times 12 + (\text{propionyl: } 2 + 3 + 1 - 1) = 30 + 72 + 5 = 107$.

**Final answer (2): net 107 ATP.**

---

## 3. Activated 15-carbon **unsaturated** fatty acid (one double bond)

Identical bookkeeping to case 2, except that the single double bond removes one acyl-CoA dehydrogenase step, hence **one FADH$_2$ fewer**:

| Source | FADH$_2$ | NADH | ATP(GTP) |
|---|---|---|---|
| 6 $\beta$-oxidation cycles, one lacking the FAD step | $6-1 = 5$ | 6 | 0 |
| 6 acetyl-CoA in TCA | 6 | 18 | 6 |
| propionyl-CoA → succinyl-CoA → OAA | 1 | 1 | 0 |
| **Total** | **12** | **25** | **6** |

**For number 3, 12 FADH$_2$ + 25 NADH + 6 ATPs are net generated.**

$$\text{ATP} = 12\times 2 + 25\times 3 + 6 = 24 + 75 + 6 = \mathbf{105}$$

(= 107 − 2, the cost of the skipped FADH$_2$.)

**Final answer (3): net 105 ATP.**

---

## 4. **Unactivated** 15-carbon **saturated** fatty acid

Same as case 2, but 2 ATP equivalents must be paid up front for acyl-CoA synthetase (ATP → AMP + 2 P$_i$):

| Source | FADH$_2$ | NADH | ATP(GTP) |
|---|---|---|---|
| 6 $\beta$-oxidation cycles | 6 | 6 | 0 |
| 6 acetyl-CoA in TCA | 6 | 18 | 6 |
| propionyl-CoA → succinyl-CoA → OAA | 1 | 1 | 0 |
| activation (−2 ATP equivalents) | – | – | −2 |
| **Total** | **13** | **25** | **4** |

**For number 4, 13 FADH$_2$ + 25 NADH + 4 ATPs are net generated.**

$$\text{ATP} = 13\times 2 + 25\times 3 + 4 = 26 + 75 + 4 = \mathbf{105}$$

**Final answer (4): net 105 ATP.**

---

## 5. **Unactivated** 15-carbon **unsaturated** fatty acid (one double bond)

Both corrections apply: one fewer FADH$_2$ (double bond) and −2 ATP (activation):

| Source | FADH$_2$ | NADH | ATP(GTP) |
|---|---|---|---|
| 6 $\beta$-oxidation cycles, one lacking the FAD step | 5 | 6 | 0 |
| 6 acetyl-CoA in TCA | 6 | 18 | 6 |
| propionyl-CoA → succinyl-CoA → OAA | 1 | 1 | 0 |
| activation (−2 ATP equivalents) | – | – | −2 |
| **Total** | **12** | **25** | **4** |

**For number 5, 12 FADH$_2$ + 25 NADH + 4 ATPs are net generated.**

$$\text{ATP} = 12\times 2 + 25\times 3 + 4 = 24 + 75 + 4 = \mathbf{103}$$

**Final answer (5): net 103 ATP.**
